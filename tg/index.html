FAQ
What is XEROVAN?

A Visa card that pays straight from your own crypto wallet. Your assets stay in your wallet, and each payment is funded at the moment you pay. No top-up.

When can I use it?

We invite people from the top of the waitlist first. We'll message you here and email you when your invite is ready.

How much does it cost?

Pricing will be announced before launch. Any fee is shown before you confirm.
